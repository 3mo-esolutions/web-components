## Columns

A column is an element in the grid, one per kind of value - `mo-data-grid-column-text`, `-number`, `-currency`, `-date` and more - that shows the value at its `dataSelector` under its `heading`.

## Selection

`selectability` is `single` or `multiple`, and `selectOnClick` selects a row by a click anywhere on it. The selected rows are `selectedData`, and each change fires `selectionChange`.

<Description of={DataGridStories.Selection} />
<Canvas of={DataGridStories.Selection} />

## Details and sub rows

A row can open into details below it. `multipleDetails` keeps more than one open, and `detailsOnClick` opens them with a click on the row.

## Editing

`editability` is `cell`, where a double click or Enter edits a cell until Escape or a click elsewhere, or `always`. Each change fires `cellEdit`.

<Description of={DataGridStories.Editing} />
<Canvas of={DataGridStories.Editing} />

## Sorting and reordering

Rows follow either a sort or an order of your own: they can be dragged only while nothing is sorted and no row has details or sub rows.

<DocsStory of={DataGridStories.Reordering} />

## Toolbar, filters and actions

The toolbar holds fields such as a search in its `toolbar` slot, filters behind a toggle in `filter`, icon buttons in `toolbar-action` and the main action in `primary-action`. Actions on a row go into its context menu.

## Large data

The grid renders cells only for the rows near the viewport, so it scrolls through thousands. `pagination='pages'` pages them instead, as many to a page as fit its height, or `pages 50` by fifty.

## In a card

`@3mo/data-grid-card-integration`, which `@3mo/del` imports, drops the padding of a `mo-card` around a grid inside it, so the grid spans the card's full width. It keeps room above the toolbar of a card without a heading, and below the grid when other content follows it.

`DataGridController` carries everything `mo-data-grid` does except its design, so a grid of another design only has to render. Here it drives a plain `<table>` in the system colors.
