## Typing in segments

A date is typed into one segment per unit, in the order and with the separators of the page's language. Shortcuts and pasted text resolve to a whole date.

## The calendar

The calendar opens from the button or with Alt+ArrowDown, beside presets such as today or the end of the month. Whatever the field refuses is disabled in it.

## Precision

`precision` picks a year, a month, a week or a day. The [Date Time Field](?path=/docs/inputs-date-time-fields-date-time-field--overview) goes on to the hour, the minute and the second.

## Ranges and times

The [Date Range Field](?path=/docs/inputs-date-time-fields-date-range-field--overview) and the [Date Time Range Field](?path=/docs/inputs-date-time-fields-date-time-range-field--overview) take a start and an end, each in its own segments. The [Time Field](?path=/docs/inputs-date-time-fields-time-field--overview) holds a time of day as the `HH:mm` string of a native time input.

## Custom fields

Each field's behaviour is a controller - `FieldDateTimeController`, `FieldDateTimeRangeController` and `FieldTimeController` - so a field of another design only renders it. The range and time pages show one of each.
