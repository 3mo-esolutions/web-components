## Panes

The `master` slot is always visible; the `detail` slot follows its state, such as its selection. The layout only reacts to the detail slot filling or emptying, so whether a detail pane is rendered at all stays your decision, and nothing is reserved while it is empty.

<DocsStory of={MasterDetailStories.Selection} />
<DocsStory of={MasterDetailStories.SideBySide} />

## Collapsing

`collapsed` keeps the detail pane's header reachable instead of removing it. Bind it to whatever collapses inside the pane, such as a `mo-collapsible-card`.

<DocsStory of={MasterDetailStories.CollapsibleDetail} />

## Any content

Neither pane has to be a 3MO component: the layout sizes whatever it is handed.
