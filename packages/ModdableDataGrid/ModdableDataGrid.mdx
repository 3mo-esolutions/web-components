## A grid of your own

`ModdableDataGrid` has no element of its own: a subclass gives it its columns in `columnsTemplate`, its filters in `toolbarDefaultTemplate`, bound to `parameters`, and its `fetch`, as the code of the grid above shows.

## Views

A view saves the grid's `parameters`, sorting, columns and pagination under a name, and selecting it applies them again. Views can be reordered by dragging their chips, copied, edited and moved to an archive.

## Storage

The views are kept by the grid's `modesAdapter`, which implements `ModdableDataGridModesAdapter` and can store them anywhere, on a server for instance.
