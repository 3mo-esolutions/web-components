## Presentations

`presentations` lists the ways to present the navigations, most preferred first, and the first one that fits is shown: the bar while its navigations fit its row, the rail while the page keeps `--mo-navigation-min-content-size` beside it, and the drawer, which always fits. Leaving one out of the list is all it takes to never show it.

## Navigations

`navigations` takes objects implementing `INavigation`: a label, an optional icon, and either children or a `link` directive that makes an element navigate, which is where your router comes in. `current` tells which navigation the page belongs to.
