## Placement

`placement` picks the edge: `block-end` by default, `block-start`, or `inline-start` and `inline-end`, which follow the writing direction. A [Drawer](?path=/docs/layout-drawer--overview) is a sheet at an inline edge, sized for navigation.

<DocsStory of={SheetStories.Placements} />

## Opening and closing

`open` opens the sheet as a modal native dialog, and `openChange` reports each change. Before the sheet closes itself it fires a cancelable `requestClose`, whose `source` is `escape`, `backdrop`, `handle`, `gesture` or `api`.

<DocsStory of={SheetStories.PreventedClose} />

## Content

The content scrolls within the sheet, and anything opened from it stays interactive above it.
