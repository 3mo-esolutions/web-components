## Items

The nesting is the hierarchy: a `mo-tree-item` inside another is one of its children. Which items are open is each item's own `open`, whoever sets it, and it reports every change as `openChange`.

## Selection

The selection belongs to the tree, which reports it in `value` by the `value` of the selected items and fires `change` when it changes.
